04. Journey

Experience & education

Jan 2021 — Dec 2021

Matric & Science Expo

Ramogabudi Senior Secondary School

Completed my matric year while participating in the Science Expo, where I created a magnetic generator project. The project gave me early hands-on exposure to experimentation, problem-solving, understanding scientific principles, documenting ideas, and turning a concept into a working demonstration.

Jun 2022 — Dec 2022

Order Taking & Restaurant Operations

McDonald's Mokopane

Worked in order taking, developing customer-service, communication, accuracy, and teamwork skills in a fast-paced environment. I also learned other operational stations, which strengthened my adaptability and willingness to learn responsibilities beyond my initial role.

Jan 2023 — Jun 2026

Computer Science Diploma

Tshwane University of Technology (TUT)

Completed my Computer Science Diploma while building a strong foundation in software development, databases, object-oriented programming, data structures and algorithms, web development, systems analysis, and software engineering. Through academic and personal projects, I developed practical full-stack and backend experience and worked with technologies including Java, Python, Spring Boot, JavaScript, Node.js, PostgreSQL, REST APIs, Git, GitHub, JWT authentication, and Docker.

During this period, I also created Neuropulse Inc. as an enterprise initiative, with TaskFlow as its first product.

2026 — Present

Software Development Internship

Tshwane University of Technology (TUT), Polokwane · ICEP

Currently gaining real-world industry experience through my internship at TUT, working under ICEP. My work includes contributing to practical software projects such as the Limpopo Varsity Hackathon (LVH) application portal and the Lecture Room Booking Management System. I am also working toward additional professional certificates while continuing to strengthen my development, collaboration, and problem-solving skills. More projects and professional milestones are to come.
